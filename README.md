## 🧠 Project Overview  
**Strategic Insights for GoodCabs** is an interactive Power BI analytics project designed to visualize operational performance, passenger behavior, and revenue trends for **GoodCabs**, a cab service operating across multiple Indian cities.  

This project demonstrates how advanced data modeling, DAX measures, and intuitive dashboards can transform static data into dynamic insights that drive **data-informed business decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective  
To enable management to monitor and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) such as:  
- Total trips and fare revenue  
- Passenger satisfaction and retention  
- Driver ratings and city-level performance  
- Target achievement for trips and passengers  

These insights empower GoodCabs to identify growth opportunities, optimize service efficiency, and enhance customer experience.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Highlights  

### 🏠 **Home Page**
A clean, interactive navigation panel connecting all report sections:
- **Overview**
- **Target & Rating**
- **Service Analysis**

---

### 📈 **Overview Page**
**Key Metrics:**  
- 🧾 **426K** Total Trips | 💰 **₹108.2M** Total Fare | 🚗 **8M km** Distance Traveled  
- **Average Fare per Trip:** ₹254 | **Avg Distance:** 19.13 km  
- **Top Cities:** Jaipur, Lucknow & Surat  
- Weekday trips contribute **56%** of total revenue  
- Monthly and passenger-type trends visualized for business insight  

---

### 🎯 **Target & Rating Page**
- 👥 **238K Total Passengers**  
- 🆕 **177K New Trips**  
- 🔁 **61K Repeat Passengers** *(25.7% retention rate)*  
- 📉 Month-over-month **Trip & Passenger Target vs Actual** performance  
- 🌆 **City-level repeat passenger** analysis  
- ⭐ **Driver Rating:** 7.83 | 🎯 **Passenger Target Rating:** 7.66  

Visual tools on this page translate raw KPIs into business intelligence — helping optimize operations and enhance satisfaction.

---

### 🧭 **Service Page**
- Comparative analysis of **Driver vs Passenger Ratings** across 10 cities  
- **Geo Map View** for fare and trip distribution  
- **Repeat Passenger Trend** analysis showing loyalty across months  

---

## 💡 Key Insights  

| Category | Insight |
|-----------|----------|
| **Performance Leaders** | Jaipur and Kochi show strong results in both revenue and ratings |
| **Customer Loyalty** | Repeat passengers contribute **over 41%** of total trips |
| **Revenue Mix** | Weekday trips drive 56% of total revenue |
| **Retention Focus** | 25.7% of passengers are repeat users — strong base for loyalty programs |
| **Optimization Opportunity** | City-level performance variations highlight areas for marketing and service improvement |

## 📈 Outcome  
This Power BI solution transforms static trip and passenger data into **a strategic decision-support system**.  
It helps GoodCabs:
- Track **revenue and target achievement**  
- Measure **passenger satisfaction trends**  
- Understand **repeat usage and retention**  
- Drive **data-informed operational improvements**

> 🎯 The dashboard empowers teams to make **faster, smarter, and customer-centric** business decisions.
